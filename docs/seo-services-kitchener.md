## H2: What SEO actually is (and why local SEO is different)

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization: the discipline of making your website and Google Business Profile visible to the people searching for what you offer. In Kitchener, the searches that matter for most local businesses are things like `plumber kitchener`, `dentist waterloo`, or `insurance broker kitchener`. These are local searches, and Google treats them differently from national searches.

For local searches, Google displays two things: the map pack (the three businesses shown with a map at the top of the results) and the organic results (the standard blue links below). Ranking in the top three of either drives the vast majority of clicks. Ranking in both is the outcome we work toward for every client.

Local SEO uses different signals than national SEO. Google looks at your Google Business Profile completeness, your reviews, your citations across directories (Yellow Pages, Bing Places, industry-specific listings), your local relevance signals (city names in your titles, your address consistency across the web, embedded maps), and your website's authority (backlinks, content, technical health). We work every one of these levers as part of a single connected process.

### H3: Technical SEO

Before we push content and links at your site, we make sure Google can crawl and index every page cleanly. That means auditing sitemap coverage, fixing canonical tag issues, resolving redirect chains, tightening page speed and Core Web Vitals, cleaning up broken links, adding structured data (LocalBusiness schema, breadcrumbs, product/service markup), and making sure your robots.txt and meta robots tags aren't accidentally blocking pages you want indexed. Most Kitchener businesses we onboard have at least three technical issues silently costing them rankings. We fix them in the first two weeks.

### H3: On-page SEO and content

Every page on your site should have a keyword-targeted title tag, an H1 that matches search intent, H2 subheadings that cover the topic thoroughly, meta descriptions that drive clicks from the SERP, and body copy long enough and helpful enough to earn the ranking. For local Kitchener businesses, that also means a dedicated landing page for the city you serve, area-specific pages if you cover multiple Ontario cities, and a service page for each core service you offer (with real content, not one paragraph and a form). We write all of this in-house.
