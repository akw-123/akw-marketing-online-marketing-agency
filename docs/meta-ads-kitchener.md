## H2: How Meta Ads differ from Google Ads

Different jobs, same funnel:

Google Ads equals intent-based. Someone searches, your ad shows up, they click. High conversion rate, higher cost per click, works best for known-brand or ready-to-buy searches.

Meta Ads equals audience-based. You define who should see your ad by demographics, interests, behaviours, or lookalike audiences. Lower cost per impression, longer buying cycle, works best for services that require awareness before conversion.

Most successful Kitchener businesses run both. Google Ads (/google-ads-kitchener) catches the ready-now demand. Meta Ads builds the pipeline of people who will be ready-now in 2-8 weeks.

## H2: Our Meta Ads services in Kitchener

### H3: Facebook and Instagram campaign strategy

Every engagement starts with a funnel map: cold audiences (they've never heard of you), warm audiences (they've engaged but not converted), and hot audiences (site visitors, cart abandoners, past customers). Each audience gets a different creative, message, and objective. Skipping this step is why most Kitchener businesses see Meta Ads underperform.

### H3: Audience targeting for the Kitchener market

We build audiences three ways: location-based geotargeting around Kitchener-Waterloo with radius overlays for adjacent cities you serve; interest-based targeting from Meta's data (parents in Kitchener, homeowners in KW, business owners in Ontario); and lookalike audiences built from your existing customer list, past leads, or website traffic. Lookalikes are almost always the highest-ROAS audience for established KW businesses.

### H3: Creative production

Meta Ads live and die on creative. We produce ad-native creative (not repurposed website images) in the formats Meta actually rewards: static images optimized for mobile feed, short-form vertical video, carousel ads for multi-product businesses, and Stories/Reels-format for younger demographic categories. We test 3-5 creative variants per campaign to identify winners fast.

### H3: Landing pages and conversion tracking

Meta Ads sending traffic to a poorly-converting landing page is like pouring water into a bucket with a hole. We audit and optimize the landing page each campaign points to, and install full Meta Pixel plus Conversions API tracking so post-iOS-14 attribution stays clean. Every conversion (form fill, purchase, appointment) maps back to the ad, creative, and audience that drove it.

### H3: Retargeting funnels

Site visitors who don't convert on the first visit are 5-10x more likely to convert with a retargeting nudge. We build retargeting audiences segmented by page depth (product-page viewers get one message, cart abandoners get another) and sequence them so each impression moves prospects one step down the funnel, not just repeats the same static ad.

### H3: Reporting and monthly optimization

Monthly reports show cost per lead, cost per acquisition, return on ad spend (ROAS), and pipeline attribution by campaign, audience, and creative. Standing weekly optimization windows (audience refresh, creative rotation, budget reallocation) keep the account from stagnating.

## H2: Frequently asked questions

### H3: Do Meta Ads still work in 2026?

Yes, better than they get credit for. Post-iOS 14 attribution changes made measurement harder, not results worse. Businesses that adapted (Conversions API, first-party data, creative discipline) still see strong ROAS. Businesses that didn't adapt see performance decay and blame "the algorithm."

### H3: Should I boost Instagram posts instead?

No. Boosted posts are the least-effective way to spend Meta ad budget. They lack proper audience targeting, funnel structure, and objective selection. A single properly-built campaign will consistently outperform 10x the same budget spent on boosts.

### H3: How is B2B Meta Ads different from B2C in Kitchener?

Longer buying cycles (4-16 weeks vs 1-4 days for consumer), narrower audiences (job-title plus industry plus company-size targeting), higher-touch creative (video testimonials, case studies), and different objectives (email captures plus retargeting sequences instead of direct conversion). We adjust our approach based on which side you're on.

### H3: Can I run Meta Ads myself?

Yes, but the learning curve is 6-12 months of paid mistakes to reach the same performance a competent manager delivers in month 2. If your ad spend is $2,000+ per month, the management fee typically pays for itself in the first month of professional-tier performance.

### H3: What if I'm also running Google Ads?

Ideal. The two channels compound. Google captures the demand Meta creates; Meta warms audiences that convert through Google branded search. We coordinate both channels' data so one channel's insights inform the other. See our Google Ads Kitchener page (/google-ads-kitchener).
