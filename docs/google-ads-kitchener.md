## H2: Why Google Ads works differently in Kitchener

Google Ads competition varies dramatically by city. In Toronto, `plumber` costs $40+ per click. In Kitchener, the same keyword runs $8 to $18. That's a 3-4x cost advantage for local Kitchener businesses that most national Google Ads agencies miss because they're optimized for national or Toronto-scale bidding.

At the same time, Kitchener's search volume for local keywords is meaningful but not massive. That means poorly-targeted broad-match campaigns waste budget on impressions from Toronto, Guelph, or Cambridge that don't convert for a Kitchener-only business. Local geotargeting, negative keywords for other cities, and location-based bid adjustments are non-negotiable for KW-focused campaigns.

We build every campaign with these local realities in the first hour of setup, not as an afterthought two months in.

## H2: Our Google Ads services in Kitchener

### H3: Search campaigns

Search campaigns capture demand from people typing what you sell into Google. We build campaigns around commercial-intent keywords (`emergency plumber kitchener`, `insurance broker near me`, `dentist accepting new patients waterloo`), write ad copy that matches search intent, and route clicks to landing pages built to convert (not to your generic homepage). Every keyword is geotargeted to Kitchener plus adjacent cities you actually serve.

### H3: Local Service Ads (LSAs)

Local Service Ads sit above the map pack for eligible business categories (home services, legal, real estate, insurance, and more). They're pay-per-lead, not pay-per-click, so you only pay when someone calls or messages. For eligible Kitchener businesses, LSAs often deliver the lowest cost per acquisition of any paid channel. We handle the Google Guarantee application, background checks, and ongoing lead-quality monitoring.

### H3: Display and remarketing

Display campaigns put your ads on relevant websites across the Google Display Network. Remarketing campaigns re-engage people who visited your site but didn't convert (they're 5-10x more likely to convert on a return visit than cold traffic). We segment audiences by page-depth, cart abandonment, and time on site so the message matches where they left off.

### H3: YouTube Ads

Video ads work when your business benefits from explaining the service (contractors, professionals, high-consideration purchases). We produce ad-format-appropriate creatives (skippable in-stream, bumpers, non-skippable), target by intent + demographic + geography, and measure through to booked appointments, not just view rate.

### H3: Shopping Ads (e-commerce clients)

For Kitchener e-commerce businesses, Google Shopping Ads show your product image, price, and store name directly in search results. We set up your Google Merchant Center feed, optimize product data, structure campaigns by margin and conversion rate, and pair with dynamic remarketing.

### H3: Conversion tracking and reporting

None of the above matters if you can't tell which clicks became customers. We install full conversion tracking (form submissions, phone calls, appointment bookings, e-commerce purchases) so every dollar spent maps back to a specific outcome. Monthly reports show cost per lead, cost per acquisition, and ROI, not vanity metrics like impressions or CTR.
