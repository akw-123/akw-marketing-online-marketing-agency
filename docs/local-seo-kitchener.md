## H2: Local SEO vs "regular" SEO — what's actually different

Traditional SEO (/seo-services-kitchener) targets organic ranking (the blue links below the map pack). Local SEO targets the map pack itself, which uses partially-different ranking signals.

The three big local ranking factors Google uses:

1. Relevance. Does your Google Business Profile (GBP) category, services, and description match what the searcher wants?
2. Distance. How close is your business to the searcher's location? (Not fully controllable, but influenceable via multi-city coverage strategies.)
3. Prominence. How authoritative is your business online — reviews, citations, backlinks, brand mentions, and website authority?

Traditional SEO wins on relevance plus prominence. Local SEO wins on all three, with a heavier weight on relevance signals from your GBP and prominence signals from local citations plus reviews.

Your GBP is the single most important asset in local SEO. We optimize it end to end:

- Primary and secondary categories chosen for maximum relevance (wrong category equals dead map pack)
- All 30+ services listed with keyword-rich 300-character descriptions
- Profile completion to full green circle (name, address, phone, hours, description, attributes, all photos)

- Geotagged photos uploaded regularly
- Q&A section seeded with common customer questions and answers
- Booking or contact links configured for direct conversion

### H3: Local citations

Citations are mentions of your business name, address, and phone number on third-party directories. Google uses citation consistency as a trust signal (matching NAP across 40+ directories equals "this business is real"). We build citations in three tiers:

- Tier 1 (80/20 must-haves): Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yellow Pages Canada, FourSquare, Data Axle. Non-negotiable.
- Tier 2 (general directories): Cylex, Hotfrog, Manta, Tupalo, Infobel, Where To, iGlobal, ShowMeLocal, and 10-20 more. Handled via LeadSnap (paid citation service) for speed.
- Tier 3 (industry-specific): trade associations, niche directories, industry aggregators. Manual outreach.

## H2: Frequently asked questions

### H3: What's the difference between local SEO and regular SEO?

Regular SEO targets the organic blue links below the map pack. Local SEO targets the map pack itself. Different ranking signals: GBP optimization, citations, reviews, and NAP consistency matter more for local; content depth and backlinks matter more for organic. ### H3: Can I rank in the map pack without a physical Kitchener address?

Only if you're a service-area business (SAB) that provides services at customer locations — plumber, contractor, insurance broker, cleaning service. Google requires you to hide your address in the GBP if you're a SAB. Businesses with no physical presence AND no service delivery in Kitchener can't legitimately rank in the Kitchener map pack.

### H3: What if I serve multiple cities (Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge)?

Your primary GBP address city is where you rank strongest. To rank in adjacent cities, you need dedicated city landing pages (like our Waterloo page at /waterloo) and consistent citations mentioning each city, but you generally can't outrank businesses physically located in those cities. Realistic expectation: dominate your primary city map pack, rank in top 5-10 for adjacent cities.

### H3: How much do reviews affect local ranking?

Significantly. Google factors both review count and rating average into local ranking. A Kitchener business with 50 reviews at 4.8 stars will typically outrank a similar business with 8 reviews at 5.0 stars. Review velocity (how often new reviews come in) also matters. We help build the systems to acquire reviews consistently, not gimmicks.
